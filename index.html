Student Finance

Study now, with your fees paid by Student Finance

If you are eligible, Student Finance pays your course fees directly to your university or college, and you can also get money for living costs. We explain how it works and help you complete the application.

Course fees

Tuition Fee Loan

Covers your course fees and is paid straight to your university or college, so you pay nothing upfront.

Living costs

Maintenance Loan

Money towards rent, travel and everyday costs, paid into your bank account in instalments during the year.

Repayment

Repay only when you earn more

You start repaying after your course, and only once your income is above the repayment threshold. Repayments come from your salary.

Who can usually apply

  • British or Irish citizensLiving in the UK for the last three years.
  • Settled status or indefinite leave to remainIncluding EU Settlement Scheme settled status.
  • Pre-settled statusMany people with pre-settled status are eligible. Some conditions apply.
  • Refugee status or humanitarian protectionAnd some other protected statuses.

Eligibility also depends on where you have lived and any earlier study. Student Finance England makes the final decision; we check your situation with you before you apply.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Your first consultation costs nothing, and we will tell you clearly about any further support before you decide.

Can I apply without A-levels?

Many courses for UK residents accept applicants with work experience instead of formal qualifications. You usually need an up-to-date CV, and some courses ask for a work reference.

How does Student Finance work?

Eligible UK students can apply for a tuition fee loan, which is paid directly to the university, and a maintenance loan to help with living costs. We help you check your eligibility and complete the application.

What is a share code?

A share code lets a university check your right to study in the UK. Many home student applications ask for one alongside your passport.

When can I start?

Many courses start in January, February, March, May, September or October, depending on the course and location. We help you choose an intake that gives you enough time to prepare your documents and Student Finance application.
