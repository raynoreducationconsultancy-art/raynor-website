Eligibility and finance

Who we can help, what documents to prepare, and how funding works.

Who can apply through us

  • British and Irish citizensLiving in the UK.
  • Settled status (ILR or EU Settlement Scheme)Living in the UK.
  • Pre-settled statusConditions apply. We check your eligibility with you.
  • Refugee status and other protected statusesConditions apply. We check your eligibility with you.

Documents you will usually need

  • Passport or national IDValid and in date.
  • Share code or eVisaIf you are not a British or Irish citizen.
  • Proof of addressA recent bank statement or utility bill.
  • National Insurance numberRequested by most institutions.
  • CV and any certificatesSome routes also ask for a work reference.
  • Personal statementWe help you plan and review it.

Finance support

How funding works

Many UK residents can apply for a loan to cover tuition fees, and support towards living costs. Eligibility depends on your residency status and any previous study.

Tuition fees

Fees for undergraduate courses can usually be covered by a government tuition fee loan, paid directly to the institution.

Living costs

Support towards living costs depends on household income and where you live while studying.

Repayment

Repayments are based on your income after your course, not on how much you borrowed.

Funding decisions are made by the government funding body, not by Raynor or the institution. We explain the process honestly, but we cannot guarantee funding. Official information: gov.uk/student-finance.

About Raynor

Where your dreams meet the destination.

Who we are

Raynor Education Consultancy Limited is an independent UK education consultancy. We help adults who live in the UK find a course that fits their goals and their life, and we support them through every step of applying.

We are not a university or an awarding body. Admissions decisions are made by each institution, and qualifications are awarded by the university named on your course.

Our standards

  • We give honest, personalised advice and never promise outcomes we do not control.
  • We tell you in writing about any cost before you commit.
  • We never write application materials for you.
  • We protect your personal data and share it only with your permission.
  • We handle complaints quickly and fairly.

Home / Privacy notice

Privacy notice

Raynor Education Consultancy Limited ("we") is responsible for the personal information you give us.

What we collect

Your name, contact details, course interests and, if you choose to apply through us, the documents and information an institution needs.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry and, with your agreement, to support your application. We share your details with an institution only when you ask us to apply on your behalf. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to support you, or as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@raynoreducation.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Complaints

We want every person we help to be treated fairly. If you are unhappy with our service, please tell us.

How to complain

  • Email info@raynoreducation.co.uk with your name, contact details and what went wrong.
